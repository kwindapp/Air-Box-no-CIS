Porsche 911 SC Airbox – Time for a Modern Solution!

The original Porsche 911 SC airbox is a well-known weak point. Made using 1970s plastic technology, these parts are now over 40 years old and prone to cracking, especially from intake backfires.

What's even crazier are the prices for replacement airboxes. Even used, repaired, or aftermarket units can cost ridiculous amounts of money.

With today's 3D printing technology and modern high-temperature materials, there should be a better, stronger, and more affordable solution.

That's why I've started designing my own custom airbox for my RWB Porsche 911 SC with electronic fuel injection.

The ultimate goal would be a modular design: one version fully compatible with the original Bosch K-Jetronic system and factory air filter, and another simplified version for K-Jetronic delete conversions with modern electronic fuel injection.

One modular platform, multiple configurations, and potentially a much more affordable solution for the entire Porsche G-model community.
